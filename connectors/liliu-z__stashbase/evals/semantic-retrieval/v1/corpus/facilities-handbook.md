# Building facilities handbook

This handbook collects the standing arrangements for the main office. Each
section is maintained by the facilities coordinator and reviewed once a year.
Ask in the facilities channel when a situation is not described here rather
than improvising, because several of these arrangements exist to satisfy the
building owner's own obligations.

## Entry badges and visitors

Every employee carries a proximity badge that opens the lobby turnstiles and
the floor doors between seven in the morning and eight in the evening. Outside
those hours the turnstiles require a badge and a personal code. Visitors sign
in at the lobby desk, receive a temporary badge that expires the same evening,
and stay with the person who invited them. A lost badge is deactivated as soon
as it is reported; replacements take one working day and are collected from the
facilities coordinator in person.

## Deliveries and post

Parcels arrive at the loading bay behind the building and are brought to the
post room on the second floor twice a day. Perishable deliveries are refused
unless the recipient has arranged collection in advance, because the post room
is unattended overnight. Outgoing post leaves at four in the afternoon.
Anything that needs tracked shipping is logged in the register beside the post
room door with the sender's name and the destination.

## Leaving the building when the alarm sounds

A continuous siren means everyone leaves immediately by the nearest staircase.
Do not gather belongings, do not use the lifts, and do not return for a
colleague you have not seen — report them missing instead. Floor wardens wear
high-visibility vests and sweep the meeting rooms, the quiet booths, and the
toilets before they follow the last person down. The assembly point is the far
corner of the north car park, past the cycle shelter, where wardens count their
floor against the badge-in register and report to the building manager. Nobody
re-enters until the building manager says the alarm is cleared, even when the
siren stops on its own.

## Rooms and desks

Meeting rooms are booked through the shared calendar and are released
automatically when nobody checks in within ten minutes. The two soundproof
booths are never bookable and are taken in turn. Desks on the third floor are
unassigned; clear yours at the end of the day so the cleaning team can reach
the surface.

## Parking and cycles

The car park has thirty spaces, six of them reserved for accessible parking and
four for electric charging. Spaces are allocated by a monthly ballot. The cycle
shelter is behind the loading bay, is covered, and holds forty bicycles; the
shelter door uses the same badge as the lobby.
