# New joiner handbook

Everything a new colleague needs in the first month, in the order it usually
comes up. Your manager walks through the first two sections with you; the rest
you can read when the relevant moment arrives.

## Your first day

Collect your laptop from the IT desk on the ground floor. It arrives with the
standard image, disk encryption already enabled, and a temporary password that
you replace during the first sign-in. Register a second factor before you leave
the desk, because most internal tools refuse a single-factor session. The IT
desk also issues a headset and, on request, an external keyboard and a stand.

## Choosing your benefits

The benefits window stays open for thirty days from your start date. Inside
that window you choose a health plan tier, decide whether to add dependants,
and set your pension contribution rate. Outside the window the choices are
fixed until the annual enrolment period or a qualifying life event. The
benefits handbook in the shared drive lists what counts as a qualifying event.

## Asking for time away from work

Holiday is requested in the people system, not by message, so that the balance
and the approval stay in one place. Enter the first and last day away, pick the
type of absence, and submit it to your manager, who normally answers within two
working days. Requests longer than two consecutive weeks, or any request that
overlaps a release window, need a second approval from the department lead.
Cancel a request you no longer need rather than letting it lapse, because the
balance is only returned when the entry is withdrawn. Sickness is recorded the
same way but afterwards, on the first day back.

## Spending company money

Small purchases go on the team card held by your manager. Anything recurring,
anything over the departmental limit, and any new supplier relationship goes
through the procurement request form first. Keep the itemised receipt; a card
statement line is not enough for the finance team to reconcile the entry.

## Finding your feet

Every new colleague is paired with a mentor outside their immediate team for
the first three months. The pairing is deliberately loose: a standing half hour
each fortnight, and an open channel for the questions that feel too small to
ask anywhere else.
