# Rain-season field manual

Guidance for teams working through the wet months. Sections are ordered by how
early in a trip they usually matter.

## Keeping equipment dry

Store radios, cameras, and spare batteries in sealed dry bags with fresh
desiccant packets. Open the bags only inside shelter, wipe condensation from
every surface before packing, and keep wet clothing in a separate compartment
so it cannot wick moisture back into the sealed items. Silica packets that have
changed colour are spent and must be swapped at the next resupply rather than
dried over a stove.

## Crossing swollen water

Scout upstream and downstream before entering any watercourse that has risen
since the last visit. Unclip chest and waist straps so a pack can be shed, face
upstream, and move sideways with a planted third point of contact. Turn back
when the water reaches mid-thigh or when the bed cannot be seen; a crossing
that has to be argued for is a crossing that should be abandoned.

## Choosing a campsite

Pitch above the visible flood line and away from single standing trees and from
the outside bend of a channel. Clear the drainage line around the tent before
the evening rain rather than during it, and keep the entrance facing away from
the prevailing wind.

## Field repairs

Carry adhesive fabric patches, a short length of cord, and spare buckles. Dry
and warm a tear before patching it; a patch applied to a wet surface lifts
within a day. Log every repair so the equipment is checked properly at the end
of the season.
