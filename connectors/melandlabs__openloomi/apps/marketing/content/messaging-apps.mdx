# Messaging Apps

<img
  src="/img/pic/IM.png"
  alt="OpenLoomi main window — Telegram, Lark/Feishu, QQ, iMessage, and WhatsApp floating around the app"
/>

Besides using the OpenLoomi interface, you can also interact with OpenLoomi directly through your favorite messaging apps. Once connected, OpenLoomi becomes your AI assistant within those platforms.

<Callout type="warning">
  All conversations and data are stored **locally** on your device. The app must
  remain **open** for the messaging-app feature.
</Callout>

## Goal

By the end of this page you should be able to:

1. Pick the messaging app that fits your workflow (bot-token vs user-account).
2. Authorize OpenLoomi inside that app and confirm a round-trip reply.
3. Use the same chat to set reminders, run commands, or trigger automations.

## Before you begin

- OpenLoomi Desktop is installed and the local API on `127.0.0.1:3414` (or `:3515`) is reachable.
- The platform-specific authorization is ready — QR code / phone / bot token / app password depending on the platform.
- The desktop app stays open during normal use; reminders and pushes route through it.

## Setting Up

1. Go to **Settings → Connectors**.
2. Click **Connect** on your preferred messaging platform.
3. Follow the authorization steps (OAuth, QR code, or device pairing).
4. Start chatting with OpenLoomi.

For detailed setup instructions for each platform, see the [Connectors guide](/docs/connectors).

## Verify

- The connector shows **Connected** in **Settings → Connectors**.
- A message you send yourself (Telegram Saved Messages, WhatsApp self-chat) returns an AI reply in the same thread.
- A bot-platform user who DMs your bot gets an AI response within a few seconds.

## Troubleshooting

- **No reply arrives.** Confirm the desktop app is still running and the local API port is reachable.
- **Bot doesn't reply in a group chat.** Bots only respond when @mentioned; they do not passively read all group traffic.
- **iMessage connector disconnected.** Re-grant Full Disk Access and Automation permissions, then restart the app.
- **Bot doesn't appear in Lark/Feishu.** Confirm the app was published in the enterprise admin console; unpublished apps can't be searched.
