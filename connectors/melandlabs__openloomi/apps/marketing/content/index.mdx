# Welcome

OpenLoomi is an open-source AI coworker you run on your own machine. It connects the apps you already use, builds a holistic context of your people, projects, and decisions, and tells you what happened, why it matters, and what to do next — saving your attention for what matters.

<Callout type="info">
  **Fastest path:** if you only do one thing, [install the desktop
  app](/docs/getting-started), authorize one connector (Gmail or Slack is the
  most useful), and let Loop run for one afternoon. Most readers finish a first
  **Approve** within 30 minutes. Already living in Claude Code or Codex? Skip
  the desktop and [install the plugin](/docs/plugins) instead.
</Callout>

## Overview

The docs are grouped so you only read what you need:

- **Start here** — the first 10 minutes: install, sign in, and finish a session.
- **Use OpenLoomi** — the day-to-day surfaces: connectors, messaging apps, automation, chat, memory, and the Loop.
- **Build / Integrate** — plugins, skills, and the library for developers extending OpenLoomi.
- **Reference** — agent runtimes, the openloomi-ctl CLI, embedding providers, privacy & security, benchmarks, and the changelog.
